QuickStack ("we," "us," or "our") provides a personal comic collection management application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

By using the Service, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate the Service:

Account Information

  • Email address

User Content

  • Comic collection data (titles, issue numbers, notes, condition, value, etc.)
  • Wishlist data
  • Cover images you upload or capture

Technical and Usage Information

  • Session data (login timestamps, authentication tokens)
  • Device and connection information (such as IP address and basic device/browser type) required for security and service operation

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or behavioral tracking data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Create and maintain your account

  • Respond to support requests

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or data resale.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Supabase, a third-party cloud infrastructure provider.

  • Data is transmitted over HTTPS
  • Passwords are securely hashed

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Camera and Photo Access

The app may request access to your device camera solely to allow you to capture comic cover images.

  • Photos are uploaded only when you choose to do so
  • We do not access your photo library without your explicit action
  • We do not use your images for any purpose outside of providing the Service

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data.

We may share data only in the following cases:

Service Providers

We use trusted third-party providers (such as Supabase) to host and process data on our behalf. These providers may process your data solely to provide infrastructure and are contractually obligated to protect it.

We may disclose information if required by law or in response to valid legal requests.

6. International Data Processing

By using the Service, you consent to this transfer.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active.

If you delete your account:
