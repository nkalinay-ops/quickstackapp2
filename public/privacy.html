We collect only the information necessary to operate, secure, and support the Service.

Account Information

We may collect:

  • Email address

  • Account identifiers associated with your QuickStack account

User Content

We may collect the information you choose to add to the Service, including:

  • Comic collection data, such as titles, issue numbers, notes, condition, estimated value, and related details

  • Other information you voluntarily enter into the app

Billing and Subscription Information

If you purchase a paid feature, subscription, or other digital product through the app, payment processing is handled by Google Play Billing and, if applicable, our subscription management provider, RevenueCat.

We may receive and store limited billing-related information needed to manage your access to paid features, such as:

  • Product or subscription identifier
  • Purchase status

  • Renewal, cancellation, expiration, refund, or grace-period status
  • Google Play order ID or purchase token
  • App user ID or account identifier used to associate the purchase with your QuickStack account

We do not collect or store your full credit card number, debit card number, bank account number, CVV, or Google Play payment credentials. Those payment details are processed by Google Play or the applicable payment provider.

Technical and Usage Information

We may collect limited technical information necessary for security, authentication, and service operation, including:

  • Session data, such as login timestamps and authentication tokens
  • Device and connection information, such as IP address and basic device or browser type

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or behavioral tracking data for advertising or profiling purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Store and display your collection and wishlist
  • Enable image upload and retrieval
  • Authenticate users and maintain security
  • Send password reset, account, or service-related emails
  • Respond to support requests
  • Process, verify, and manage purchases or subscriptions
  • Provide or restore access to paid features
  • Handle billing support, refunds, cancellations, chargebacks, and fraud-prevention needs
  • Comply with legal, tax, accounting, and platform requirements

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or data resale.

3. Billing and Subscription Management

Purchases and subscriptions made through the Android app are processed through Google Play Billing. Google Play may collect and process payment information according to Google's own terms and privacy policies.

QuickStack may use RevenueCat or a similar subscription management provider to help verify purchases, manage subscription status, restore purchases, and determine whether your account has access to paid features.

If you cancel or delete your QuickStack account, this does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. You are responsible for managing or canceling your subscription through your Google Play account or the Google Play subscription management tools.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Supabase, a third-party cloud infrastructure provider.

  • Data is transmitted over HTTPS
  • Passwords are securely hashed
  • Images are stored in private storage associated with your account
  • Billing-related entitlement records are stored only as needed to operate paid features, support purchases, and meet legal or platform requirements

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Camera and Photo Access

The app may request access to your device camera solely to allow you to capture comic cover images.

  • Photos are uploaded only when you choose to do so
  • We do not access your photo library without your explicit action
  • We do not use your images for any purpose outside of providing the Service
