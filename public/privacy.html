QuickStack

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 1, 2026  |  Last Updated: May 1, 2026

QuickStack ("we," "us," or "our") is a personal comic collection tracker application. We are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about the data we collect. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the QuickStack service:

  • Account Information: Your email address and encrypted password, used to create and secure your account.
  • Comic Collection Data: The details of comics you add to your collection (titles, issue numbers, notes, condition, value, and similar metadata you choose to enter).
  • Wishlist Data: Comics you add to your wishlist.
  • Cover Images: Photos you take or upload as comic cover images. These are stored in your account and are only accessible to you.
  • Usage Information: Basic session data (login timestamps, session tokens) necessary to keep your account secure and operational. We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or behavioral tracking data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Store and display your comic collection and wishlist
  • Send password reset emails when you request them
  • Authenticate your identity and maintain session security
  • Respond to support requests

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the QuickStack service.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database platform that provides encrypted storage and industry-standard security practices. All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

Cover images are stored in Supabase Storage and are associated with your account. Only you can access your images through the app.

4. Camera and Photo Access

The QuickStack app requests access to your device camera solely to allow you to photograph comic covers for your collection. Photos you take are uploaded directly to your private account storage. We do not access your device's photo library without your explicit action of selecting an image. We do not share your photos with any third party.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for commercial purposes. We may disclose your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Supabase, our database and storage provider, processes your data on our behalf under their own privacy and security policies.
  • Legal Requirements: If required to do so by law or in response to valid legal process.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account through the app's Settings screen, all of your data — including your comic collection, wishlist, and cover images — is permanently deleted from our systems. This process is irreversible.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: You can view all data associated with your account within the app at any time.
  • Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings > Danger Zone > Delete Account.
  • Correction: You can update your password and other account details from the Settings screen.
  • Portability: Contact us to request an export of your data.

8. Children's Privacy

QuickStack is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
