# How to Create Your Account

*This article explains how to create a QuickStack account on Android.*

QuickStack accounts are created through the Android app. You cannot sign up on the website — the website is for viewing an existing collection only.

## Before you start

- Download QuickStack from the Google Play Store.
- Have your email address ready.

## Steps

1. Open the QuickStack app.
2. On the sign-in screen, tap **Don't have an account? Sign Up**.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
4. Create a password. Your password must be at least 12 characters long.
5. Enter your password again in the **Confirm Password** field.
6. Tap **Create Account**.
7. Check your email inbox for a confirmation message from QuickStack.
8. Tap the link in the confirmation email to activate your account.
9. Return to the app and sign in with your email and password.

## Tips

- If you don't see the confirmation email within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder.
- Your password must meet the minimum length requirement of 12 characters. A strength indicator will show you when your password is strong enough.
- By creating an account, you agree to the QuickStack Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, which are linked on the sign-up screen.

# Adding Your First Comic

*This article explains how to add your first comic to your QuickStack collection.*

You can add a comic to your collection by scanning the cover with your camera, or by typing the details manually. Scanning is the fastest way.

## Using the camera to scan a cover

1. Tap the **+** button in the bottom navigation bar to open the Add Comic screen.
2. Make sure **Collection** is selected at the top of the screen (not Wishlist).
3. Tap **Scan Comic Cover**.
4. Point your camera at the front cover of the comic. Hold it steady.
5. Tap the shutter button to capture the photo.
6. Wait a moment while QuickStack reads the cover. The fields below will fill in automatically.
7. Review the filled-in details. Tap any field to correct it if something is wrong.
8. Optionally, select a **Condition** for the comic (Mint, Near Mint, Very Fine, and so on).
9. Tap **Add to Collection**.

The comic is now saved and will appear in your collection.

## Typing details manually

If you prefer not to scan, you can skip the camera step and fill in the fields by hand.

1. Tap the **+** button to open the Add Comic screen.
2. Type the series name in the **Series** field. This field is required.
3. Fill in any other fields you know: **Story**, **Issue #**, **Publisher**, **Year**, **Condition**, and **Notes**.
4. Tap **Add to Collection**.

# How to Scan a Comic Cover

*This article explains how to use the camera to scan a comic cover and add it to your collection.*

Scanning lets QuickStack read the title, issue number, publisher, year, and other details directly from the cover. You do not need to type anything.

## Steps

1. Tap the **+** button in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Confirm that **Collection** is selected at the top (or tap **Wishlist** if you want to add to your wishlist instead).
3. Tap **Scan Comic Cover**.
4. The camera opens. Hold the comic cover flat and square in front of the camera.
5. Tap the shutter button to take the photo.
6. The app will close the camera and show "Analyzing comic cover..." for a moment.
7. When analysis is complete, the form fields fill in automatically. Fields that were filled by the scan are highlighted in blue.
8. Review each field. If any detail is wrong, tap that field and correct it.
9. Select the **Condition** if you want to record it.
10. Tap **Add to Collection** to save the comic.

Your photo is saved alongside the comic entry so you can see the cover in your collection.

## Scanning for your wishlist

If you scan a cover while in **Wishlist** mode, the details are still extracted from the cover, but the photo is not stored. Wishlist entries do not have cover images.

## Scan Next

After saving a comic, you can tap **Save & Scan Next** instead of **Add to Collection**. This saves the current comic and immediately opens the camera again — useful when adding a stack of comics at once.

# Tips for Better Scan Accuracy

*This article explains how to get the most accurate results when scanning comic covers.*

QuickStack reads text and details from your cover photo. Better photos produce better results.

## Lighting

- Scan in a well-lit area. Natural light or bright indoor light both work well.
- Avoid scanning in dim rooms or with harsh shadows across the cover.
- If your cover has a glossy finish, tilt it slightly to avoid direct reflections.

## Camera angle and distance

- Hold the comic flat and face-on to the camera. Angled shots reduce accuracy.
- Keep the entire cover in the frame — do not crop out the title or issue number.
- Keep a distance of about 20–30 cm (8–12 inches) from the cover.

## Cover condition

- Severely worn or faded covers may be harder to read. Try manual entry for heavily damaged comics.
- Covers with large stickers or stamps covering the title may not scan correctly. Remove stickers if possible, or enter details manually.

## What to do if results are wrong

- Review all filled-in fields before saving. Tap any field to correct it.
- Save the corrected version. QuickStack learns from your corrections over time and will apply them automatically on future scans of the same series.

# What to Do When a Comic Isn't Recognized

*This article explains what to do if a scan does not produce the correct results.*

Sometimes the app cannot confidently identify a comic from the cover photo. This can happen with very old comics, unusual cover layouts, or poor lighting.

## If some fields are missing or wrong

The scan may still return partial results. The app shows a notice that says "Could not extract all details from the image." In this case:

1. Fill in any missing fields by tapping them and typing.
2. Correct any fields that look wrong.
3. Tap **Add to Collection** to save.

QuickStack notes your corrections. If you correct the same scan results multiple times, the app will offer to save a rule so it can apply the correction automatically next time.

## If the scan fails entirely

The app shows an error message. Tap to dismiss it, then try one of these options:

- Tap **Scan Comic Cover** again and take a new photo with better lighting or a steadier hand.
- Enter the comic details by hand in the form below the scan button. See the article "Manually Adding a Comic by Title or Issue" for guidance.

## If the scan limit is reached

If you have used all your scans for the month, the scan button will be disabled. You can still add comics manually. Alternatively, upgrade your plan to get more scans. Your scan count resets on the first of each month.

# Manually Adding a Comic by Title or Issue

*This article explains how to add a comic to your collection by typing the details rather than scanning.*

You can add any comic without using the camera. This is useful for older comics that are hard to scan, or when you simply prefer to type.

## Steps

1. Tap the **+** button in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Choose **Collection** or **Wishlist** at the top of the screen.
3. Skip the scan button — go straight to the form fields below it.
4. Type the series name in the **Series** field. This is the only required field.
5. Fill in as many of the remaining fields as you like:
   - **Story** — the name of the story arc or storyline (optional)
   - **Issue #** — the issue number
   - **Publisher** — the publisher name (e.g., Marvel, DC)
   - **Cover Variant** — the variant number if the issue has multiple covers
   - **Total Issues in Arc** — the total number of issues in the story arc
   - **Year** — the publication year
   - **Condition** — select one from the grid: Mint, Near Mint, Very Fine, Very Good, Fine, Good, Fair, or Poor
   - **Notes** — any other details you want to record
6. Optionally, expand **Purchase Info** to record the price you paid and the date of purchase.
7. Tap **Add to Collection** (or **Add to Wishlist** if in Wishlist mode).

# Editing Comic Details and Condition

*This article explains how to edit a comic's details after it has been added to your collection.*

You can update any field on a comic entry at any time — including series name, condition, notes, cover photo, and purchase information.

## Opening a comic for editing

1. Tap **Collection** in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Browse to the comic you want to edit. You can use the **Publisher**, **Series**, or **All** browse modes, or search by name.
3. Tap the comic to open its detail view.
4. Tap **Edit Comic**.

## Editing fields

All fields become editable once you tap **Edit Comic**:

- **Series**, **Story**, **Issue Number**, **Publisher**, **Year** — tap the field and type your changes.
- **Cover Variant** — enter the variant number.
- **Condition** — tap the condition button that applies (Mint, Near Mint, Very Fine, and so on).
- **Copies Owned** — use the **+** and **-** buttons to adjust how many copies you own.
- **Total Issues in Arc** — enter the total if known.
- **Notes** — free-text notes about the comic.
- **Price Paid (USD)** and **Purchase Date** — update your purchase information.

## Replacing the cover photo

If you want to add or replace the cover photo while editing:

1. Tap **Edit Comic** to enter edit mode.
2. Tap **Replace Photo** (or the camera area if no photo exists yet).
3. Take a new photo. The photo is saved immediately.

## Saving your changes

Tap **Save Changes** when you are done. Tap **Cancel** to discard your changes without saving.

# Deleting Comics from Your Collection

*This article explains how to remove a comic from your QuickStack collection.*

Deleting a comic is permanent. There is no undo, so make sure you want to remove the entry before confirming.

## Method 1: From the detail view

1. Tap **Collection** in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Browse to the comic you want to delete.
3. Tap the comic to open its detail view.
4. Tap **Delete Comic**.
5. A confirmation dialog will appear. Tap **Delete** to confirm, or **Cancel** to go back.

## Method 2: From the list view

In the flat **All** list or issue list, each comic row has a trash icon on the right side.

1. Tap the trash icon on the comic you want to remove.
2. Confirm the deletion in the dialog that appears.

## What gets deleted

Deleting a comic removes:

- All stored details (series, issue, condition, notes, and so on)
- The cover photo associated with that entry

Your other comics are not affected.

## Managing copies instead of deleting

If you own multiple copies and want to remove just one, edit the comic and reduce the **Copies Owned** count using the **-** button, rather than deleting the entire entry.

# Importing Comics from a Spreadsheet

*This article explains how to import a large list of comics into QuickStack using a spreadsheet file.*

Bulk import is available on the **Plus** plan and can only be done from a desktop computer or laptop — it is not available in the Android app.

## Requirements

- A Plus plan subscription
- A computer with a web browser
- Your collection data in a CSV or Excel (.xlsx or .xls) file
- Maximum 1,000 rows per upload, maximum 5 MB file size

## Getting the template

Before uploading, download the official template so your columns match the expected format.

1. Sign in at [appquickstackapp.com](https://appquickstackapp.com) from a desktop browser.
2. Navigate to **Bulk Upload** in the menu.
3. Click **Download Template**.
4. Open the downloaded file in Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet app.

## Filling in the template

The only required column is **Series**. All other columns are optional:

| Column | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| Series | Required |
| Story | Story arc or subtitle |
| Issue Number | The issue number |
| Publisher | e.g., Marvel, DC |
| Year | Publication year (four digits) |
| Condition | Mint, Near Mint, Very Fine, Very Good, Fine, Good, Fair, or Poor |
| Notes | Any notes about the comic |
| Copy Count | Number of copies you own (defaults to 1) |
| Cover Variant | Variant number |
| Total Issues in Arc | Total issues in the story arc |
| Purchase Price | Price you paid (numbers only, no currency symbol) |
| Purchase Date | Format: YYYY-MM-DD |

## Uploading your file

1. On the **Bulk Upload** page, click **Select File** or drag your file into the upload area.
2. After the file appears in the panel, click **Start Upload**.
3. Wait for processing to complete. The status updates automatically.

## Reviewing results

After the upload finishes, the **Upload History** section shows how many rows were added, how many were duplicates (copy count incremented), and how many had errors.

If any rows failed, click **View errors** to see which rows had problems and why.

## Notes

- Duplicate entries (same series and issue number already in your collection) automatically increment the copy count rather than creating a new entry.
- Imported comics do not have cover photos. You can add a photo later by editing the individual comic entry.

# Cancelling Your Subscription

*This article explains how to cancel a QuickStack paid subscription.*

Subscriptions are managed by Google Play, so cancellation is done through the Google Play Store — not through the QuickStack app.

## How to cancel

1. Open the Google Play Store app on your Android device.
2. Tap your profile icon in the top right corner.
3. Tap **Payments & subscriptions**, then tap **Subscriptions**.
4. Find **QuickStack** in the list and tap it.
5. Tap **Cancel subscription** and follow the prompts.

Alternatively, in the QuickStack app:

1. Tap **Settings** in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Scroll to the **Subscription** section.
3. Tap **Manage subscription**.
4. This opens the Google Play subscription management page where you can cancel.

## What happens after you cancel

- Your paid benefits remain active until the end of the period you have already paid for (shown as "Access through" in Settings).
- After that date, your account reverts to the Free tier with 20 scans per month.
- Your comics, wishlist, and account data are not deleted when you cancel.

## Changing your mind

If you cancel and then decide to resubscribe, tap the upgrade button in **Settings** at any time to start a new subscription.

# How Scan Correction Works

*This article explains how QuickStack learns from your edits to improve scan accuracy over time.*

When you scan a comic cover and correct the results, QuickStack remembers those corrections. After you make the same correction enough times, it offers to save a rule so future scans of the same cover are fixed automatically.

## How it works

Each time you scan a cover and edit the Series, Story, or Publisher field before saving, QuickStack records the change. After you have made the same correction three times, a banner appears asking if you want to save a correction rule.

Once a rule is saved, the next time the scanner reads that same incorrect text, QuickStack replaces it automatically before showing you the form. A banner confirms what was corrected and gives you an option to undo it.

## What gets tracked

The correction engine tracks three fields:

- **Series name**
- **Story arc**
- **Publisher**

Issue number, year, condition, and other fields are not tracked by the correction engine.

## The suggestion banner

After enough corrections of the same value, a banner appears at the bottom of the Add Comic form:

> "Save a scan correction rule? You've corrected [original] to [corrected] 3 times. QuickStack can apply this automatically from now on."

You have two options:

- **Save Rule** — confirms the correction. Future scans matching the original OCR text will be corrected automatically.
- **Dismiss** — closes the banner without saving. QuickStack will not suggest it again, but you can still correct the field manually on future scans.

## The auto-correction banner

When a saved rule applies during a scan, a blue banner appears at the top of the form showing what was changed and the original text the scanner read.

- Tap **Undo** to restore the original scanned text if you want it back.
- Tap the X to dismiss the banner without making any change.

## Tips

- Corrections only count when you scan first and then edit. Manually typed entries do not contribute to correction rules.
- Wishlist scans count too. Corrections made while adding to your wishlist contribute to the same correction rules as collection scans.
- If you dismissed a suggestion by mistake, the correction data is still saved. The suggestion will reappear after your next correction of the same value crosses the threshold again.
- If an auto-correction applies to the wrong comic, tap **Undo** in the banner. That one instance will not affect the saved rule.
